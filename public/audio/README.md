# Audio files

**Owners:** Rei, Teo (backend)

Real recordings for the gramophone, and any scores (MusicXML) used to make the note text for pieces. The piano itself plays its notes with Tone.js (`js/audio.js`), so it doesn't need files here.

## Sound effects

Made by `scripts/import_sounds.py` from the recordings in `design/sounds` (free to use without credit: CC0, except the laugh, see below), played by `js/sfx.js`:

- `steps/stone-N`, `steps/wood-N`, `steps/wet-N`: footsteps (cobbles, the shop's floor, the flooded future), five of each, from Kenney's [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) (concrete, wood, grass).
- `time-travel/sparkle`, `time-travel/gust`, `time-travel/chime`: the chime as the tube opens, one 2.5 s gust of wind and a little chime, from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com) (*Chimes "Dream" #1*, *Strong wind #2*, *Chimes "Butterfly" #1*).
- `shop/meow-0` to `-2`: three small meows for the cat on the piano, from BigSoundBank (*Little meow of a cat #1*, *#2*, *#3*).
- `shop/laugh`: Meneer Simone's "ho ho ho", from [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co) (*Santa Claus Ho Ho Ho laughing*), under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License: free to use in the game without credit, but not to be shared on its own as a loose sound.
- `weather/rain`, `weather/thunder-0` to `-2`: a 20 s loop of gentle rain and three distant thunders, from BigSoundBank (*Rain Under an Umbrella*, *Thunder #2*, *#3*, *#7*).
